Use this guide to run an authorized assessment, understand the results, and keep a copy of your report.

Open the scanner ↗
01

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Choose a site you are allowed to test.

Only assess a website you own or have explicit permission to test. The scanner accepts HTTP or HTTPS URLs and blocks private, loopback, and link-local destinations by default.

Good to knowThis tool does not sign in to sites, submit credentials, exploit issues, or perform destructive tests.
02

RUN A SCAN

Enter the target and confirm authorization.

Open Scanner, enter the full website URL, check the permission confirmation, and start the assessment. Requests have timeouts, redirect limits, and response-size limits.

Stay in scopeDo not scan systems outside your permission. A scan may take several seconds depending on the target response.
03

UNDERSTAND RESULTS

Review each signal, not just the score.

Read the severity, evidence, and suggested remediation on the report. Findings are automated signals and can be false positives or false negatives; verify them and test fixes yourself.

RememberThe score is a project-specific prioritization aid, not a certification or proof that a site is secure.
04

SAVE AND RETURN

Download a report or revisit scan history.

Use the report page to download a PDF. Open Scan History to review available assessments or export records as CSV or JSON.

History storageOn the hosted site, history survives service restarts only when a persistent PostgreSQL database has been configured. Without it, free-host SQLite data can be temporary.
